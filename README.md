# Automotive Sales & Booking Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard tracking fleet booking performance, revenue, customer satisfaction, payment methods, and seasonal demand across six Toyota vehicle models to drive data-informed business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX measures, HTML/CSS/SVG custom visuals, and UI/UX design
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation
- **Excel / CSV:** Source dataset (`toyota_powerbi_dataset.csv`)

## 📊 Key Insights & Features
- **Dynamic Vehicle Filtering:** Image-based slicer allowing seamless filtering across 6 Toyota models (Prius, Hilux, RAV4, Camry, Highlander, and Corolla Cross).
- **Sales & Revenue Tracking:** Monitors total booking counts, total revenue generated (up to 16.50M for Camry), and average distance covered.
- **Customer & Rep Ratings:** Custom HTML/SVG star-rating visuals tracking Sales Rep ratings (4.4/5) alongside Customer ratings (4.4–4.5/5).
- **Operational & Payment Analytics:** Tracks booking status bottlenecks (completion rates vs. cancellations and stock gaps), payment preferences (Cash, Bank Transfer, Lease, Financing), and monthly/quarterly demand trends.
